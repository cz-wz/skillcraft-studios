Welcome to SkillCraft Studios!

SkillCraft Studios serves to provide access to activities that build technical skills for creative pursuits. It does this by providing weekly group skills classes in musical and game-based groups; by hosting educational and Q&A gatherings on executive functioning; and in promoting other skill-building classes in the local Whatcom community.
